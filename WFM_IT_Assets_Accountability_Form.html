Section B — Terms of Accountability

As an employee working from home, I understand that the asset(s) listed in Section A remain the property of Pinnacle Intelligence and are entrusted to my care. I agree to the following:

  1. Receipt of assets. I acknowledge receipt of the company-issued IT asset(s) listed above in good working condition, issued to me for use at my approved work-from-home (WFM) location.
  2. Custody & safekeeping. While the asset(s) are assigned to me, I accept full responsibility for their proper use, care, and safekeeping at home and while in transit between my home and any company site. I will keep them in a secure place, protect them from damage (e.g., water, heat, power surges, pets, and children), and will not lend them to or allow their use by household members or any other person.
  3. Authorized use only. I will use the asset(s) solely for authorized, work-related purposes. I will not install unauthorized software, disable or alter security settings, or have the asset(s) opened or repaired by anyone other than the Pinnacle IT Team.
  4. Location. I will keep the asset(s) at my approved remote work address and will not relocate them to another address without prior approval.
  5. Reporting. I will report any loss, theft, damage, or malfunction to the IT Team immediately, and no later than 24 hours after discovery, and will provide supporting documents when requested (e.g., a police report in case of theft).
  6. Inspection & return. I will make the asset(s) available for remote or onsite inspection when requested, and will return them complete with all accessories and in good condition upon request, at the end of my work-from-home arrangement, or upon separation from the company.
  7. Financial responsibility. In the event of loss, theft, or damage due to negligence or unauthorized use, I understand that I will be held financially responsible for the repair or replacement cost. I authorize Pinnacle Intelligence to recover these costs through salary deduction or from my final pay upon separation from the company.

By signing this document, I agree to comply with these terms and accept full accountability for the asset(s) issued to me.

Reminder: Any negligent act committed in the performance of your duty, whether working from home or onsite, is subject to the sanctions provided in the Code of Conduct and the Employee Handbook.
