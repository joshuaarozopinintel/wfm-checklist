HOME WORKSPACE INSPECTION CHECKLIST
Pinnacle Intelligence · Remote Work Program · A04 · v1.0 · One form per employee
#Audit ItemStandard / GuidanceResultNotes / Evidence
1. DEDICATED & PRIVATE WORKSPACE
1Separate enclosed area — not a common living areaDedicated room or partitioned area. Sofas/dining tables do not qualify.
2Can be locked or secured to prevent unauthorized entryDoor with lock preferred. Document compensating control if none.
3Household members cannot view work screen during work hoursPhysical line of sight to screen blocked from common areas.
4Used exclusively for work during scheduled hoursNo shared use with household members during work hours.
2. DESK, CHAIR & ERGONOMICS
5Dedicated desk or flat work surface presentStable surface adequate for device + peripherals. Not a bed or sofa.
6Ergonomic seating availableChair with back support. Not a stool or floor cushion.
7Monitor or laptop screen at approximately eye levelTop of screen at or slightly below eye level.
8Keyboard and mouse allow neutral wrist postureArms parallel to floor, wrists straight.
9No significant clutter obstructing workspaceClear working surface. No fire hazards near power strips.
3. LIGHTING
10Adequate lighting in workspaceNatural or artificial light clearly illuminates work surface.
11No glare on screen or camera from light sourceLight source beside or behind screen, not behind employee.
12Employee clearly visible on webcam under existing lightingTest webcam during inspection. Face must be clearly lit.
4. NOISE ENVIRONMENT
13Ambient noise acceptable for voice workBackground noise must not interfere with call quality.
14Noise-cancelling headset available and functionalCompany-issued or employee-owned NC headset. Audio tested.
15Household noise sources controlled during work hoursTV, children, pets. Employee declaration + noise test.
16No significant external noise intrusion during testAssessed during inspection. Note intermittent issues.
5. POWER & ELECTRICAL
17UPS unit present and connected (mandatory for BDC/voice)Minimum 650VA. Record model and VA rating in notes.
18UPS battery tested — minimum 15 min backupBattery test or purchase within 6 months.
19No overloaded power strips or daisy-chained extensionsMax 3 devices per strip.
20Surge protector in use for work equipmentSurge protector or UPS covers all work devices.
6. NETWORK EQUIPMENT
21Home Wi-Fi router confirmed as personal/household routerNot a shared or public connection.
22Work device connected via wired ethernet or stable Wi-FiEthernet preferred. Note if Wi-Fi only.
23Signal strength acceptable at workstationMinimum 3 bars / -70 dBm.
7. SCREEN PRIVACY
24Screen privacy filter installed on work monitorPhysical filter covering full screen. Black from side.
25Laptop screen not visible to household membersCamera test or placement confirms side/rear obscured.
8. EQUIPMENT CONDITION
26All company-issued equipment present and in good conditionCross-check against IT Asset Register.
27No visible damage to devices, cables, or peripheralsPhysical inspection.
28Serial numbers match IT Asset Register recordsCross-reference serial on device with asset record.
